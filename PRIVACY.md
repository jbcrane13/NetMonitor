# Privacy Policy

**NetMonitor Pro**
**Effective Date:** February 11, 2026

## Overview

NetMonitor Pro is a network monitoring and diagnostic tool for macOS. Your privacy matters — this app is designed to keep all data on your device.

## Data Collection

**NetMonitor Pro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.**

All data generated by the app — including monitoring results, device discovery data, network measurements, and session records — is stored locally on your Mac using Apple's SwiftData framework.

## Network Access

NetMonitor Pro accesses the network solely to perform the diagnostic functions you initiate:

- **Monitoring checks** (HTTP, ICMP, TCP) to targets you configure
- **Device discovery** via ARP and Bonjour on your local network
- **Diagnostic tools** (ping, traceroute, DNS lookup, WHOIS, port scanning, speed test) directed at hosts you specify
- **Wake on LAN** packets sent to devices you choose

No data from these operations is sent to the developer or any third party.

## Local Network Companion

NetMonitor Pro can communicate with the NetMonitor iOS app over your local network for device pairing and data sync. This communication stays entirely within your local network.

## Analytics & Tracking

NetMonitor Pro does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporting services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Third-Party Services

NetMonitor Pro does not integrate with any third-party data services.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored in your Mac's sandboxed container. You can delete all data by removing the app.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
